Meri zindagi ka bara hissa do alag duniyaon ke beech guzra hai: aik taraf classical Islami ilm ki duniya, aur dosri taraf modern technology ki tez raftaar duniya. Bahaisiyat Islamic Scholar mene 8+ saal Asst. Shariah Advisor ke tor per kaam kiya hai — aur kuch saalon se AI engineering bhi. Mera maanna hai ke aane wale daur ka sab se valuable kaam wahin hoga jahan ye dono milte hain.

## Do disciplines, aik mindset

Islami uloom parhna aap ko aik aisi cheez sikha deta hai jo technology ke liye bohat qeemti hai: **maani ki barikbeeni**. Aik lafz, aik sanad, aik mas'ala — har cheez ko ehtiyat se sambhalna parta hai, source tak trace karna parta hai, aur kabhi apni taraf se nahi gharhna parta. Yehi discipline aik trustworthy AI system ko aik confident liar se alag karti hai.

Jab mein aaj AI per kaam karta hoon — agentic systems, retrieval pipelines, large language models — toh wahi instinct sath rakhta hoon jo saalon ki Shariah advisory mein seekha tha: *bina source ke baat na karo, aur fluency ko sachai na samjho.*

## Jahan ye dono duniyain milti hain

Ye sangam theoretical nahi, practical hai:

- **Bare paimane per translation.** Aik scale per asli maani ko mukhtalif zubaanon mein le jana — ye Islamic Scholar ka masla bhi hai aur engineering ka bhi.
- **Knowledge retrieval.** AI ko verified texts mein grounded karna sources ka digital version hai.
- **Trust aur ethics.** Sadiyon purani ilmi rivayat ke pass aaj ki AI industry ko sikhane ke liye bohat kuch hai.

## Mein kis cheez ki taraf kaam kar raha hoon

Mera maqsad simple hai: aisi tools banana jo **technically excellent** bhi hon aur **jo kehte hain wo trustworthy** bhi ho. Aise tools jo logon ko asli ilm tak tez access den, bina accuracy ko speed per qurban kiye.

Ye blog wo jagah hai jahan mein ye safar share karoon ga — AI experiments, Islami uloom ke saalon ka sabaq, aur wo ideas jo dono duniyaon ke sangam per khare ho ke paida hote hain.

Parhne ke liye shukriya. Aur jald, in shā' Allāh.
